Student Personal Accident Cover

Designed for university and college students — especially during internship and industrial attachment periods. Protects against death, permanent total disablement, and medical expenses arising from accidents at your place of attachment or institution during the cover period. A must-have for every student on attachment.

Premium fromKShs. 1,500/ period
Accidental death benefit (up to KShs. 300,000)
Permanent total disablement cover
Medical expenses from accident injuries
Cover at place of work attachment
Cover within the learning institution
Flexible cover periods: 3 or 6 months
Sum insured options: KShs. 100K / 200K / 300K
Affordable premiums: KShs. 1,500, 2,500 or 3,500

Student national ID or passport number
Name and address of university / college
Student admission number
Course of study
Name of company / institution for attachment
Intended start and end date of attachment
